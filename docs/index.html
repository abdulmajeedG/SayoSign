SayoSign

A cute and very capable IPA signer that runs right on your iPhone. Sign, inject tweaks, install. No computer needed.

⬇︎ Download IPA GitHub Add to AltStore
✍️

Own signing engine

Written from scratch in C: Mach-O, CodeDirectory, CMS, DER entitlements, nested bundles, ZIP64.

🔎

Live revocation check

Asks Apple's OCSP responder whether your certificate is still alive and shows days left.

Apple ID sign-in

Free or paid developer account with 2FA. Device, certificate and profiles are made for you.

🧩

Tweak injection

.dylib, .deb (gz/xz/zstd) and .framework, powered by SayoHook, our own Substrate-compatible hooking engine.

📶

Offline install

HTTPS on localhost with a certificate authority generated on your device. No leaked keys, no servers.

🛍️

Sources

AltStore, SideStore, Feather and ESign repositories with search, screenshots and one-tap download.

Get started

  1. Download SayoSign.ipa from Releases and sign it once with any tool you already have.
  2. Import your .p12 with its .mobileprovision, or sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Open Settings → Local installer setup and trust the on-device certificate once.
  4. Import any IPA, tap Sign, then Install. 🎀
